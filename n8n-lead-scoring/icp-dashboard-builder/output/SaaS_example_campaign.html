How every line was scored

  • Words: does it use the ICP's language, or marketer-speak? Words from the average segment or the anti-ICP score low: they are real, but they belong to the wrong buyer.
  • Outcome: does it lead with the result, or with the product?
  • Voice: no em dashes, no hype words, proof over claims, within character limits. A claim the data contradicts fails here.
  • The gate: any axis under 4 and the line is cut. Cut lines stay on the page, struck through, with the reason.

Notes before launch

  • Placeholders: brand, landing page and the 30-minute review offer all need confirming. Every asset points to that one page.
  • Proof permissions: lines built on a customer's words or numbers (11 carriers to one, 6 weeks instead of 4 months, 33 countries, SD-WAN then SASE) need that customer's sign-off in a real campaign.
  • Claims kept out on purpose: fast provisioning everywhere (Ulvik: 12+ weeks in Brazil and Indonesia), API depth (Juniper: "less mature than promised"), full WAN and hardware management (not what the product does), security-first positioning (lost 3 of 3).
  • Roll-over wording: the three-year default roll-over is one customer's contract, so the copy says "can roll over", not "will".
  • Procurement is thin: only 2 intros and 2 headlines passed. The fix is procurement voices in the next win/loss interviews, not more copy.
  • In-sample: the targeting rule was fitted on the same 60 accounts it was measured on. Expect lower precision on the first external list.
