Anti-ICP · exclude

Domestic rate shoppers via tender

Accounts

Pains, desires, triggers

For Stack Consolidators only. Each line names the accounts it comes from.

Pains

  1. Every new market means another PSP. Four dashboards, four settlement files, no real approval rate.Barrow Gaming, Clyde Play, Pentland Travel
  2. An acquisition doubles the stack: same clients, two sets of rails.Inchcape Invest
  3. The card step fails where cards underperform. One deposit in three lost in LatAm. No local methods, no purchase.Belmar Play, Carrow Studios, Wynford Interactive
  4. Payouts and withdrawals take three to four days, and the team spends its week chasing failed ones.Dalby Invest, Tarrant Exchange, Esker Bet, Garve Invest, Zennor Hub
  5. FX paid twice on thin margins.Elvarn Travel, Jarrow Holidays

Desires

  1. One integration and one contract for every market.Belmar Play, Pentland Travel, Wynford Interactive
  2. Open new markets without a new integration.Clyde Play, Garve Invest
  3. Local methods where cards don't work.Belmar Play, Carrow Studios, Wynford Interactive
  4. Same-day payouts, including to countries the big providers skip.Esker Bet, Garve Invest, Zennor Hub
  5. A business case in their own numbers the CFO will sign.Garve Invest; Elvarn Travel (AE note)
  6. Fraud with a named owner, and the option to keep existing rules in year one.Fintry Exchange; Dalby Invest (AE note)

Triggers

  1. Acquired a smaller competitor, brand or broker. 7 of 17, 79 to 300 days before pipeline. Confirmed tier.Belmar, Esker, Jarrow, Fintry, Clyde, Inchcape, Zennor
  2. New markets or licences announced. 6 of 17, 60 to 282 days before. Confirmed tier.Wynford, Esker, Farrow, Tarrant, Carrow, Garve
  3. PSP contract up for renewal within 12 months. 6 of 17, 62 to 229 days before. Mostly Inferred tier.Pentland, Barrow, Garve, Fintry, Elvarn, Clyde

Anti-triggers (lost or churned every time): Head of Payments left and not backfilled (3 of 3). New Head of Risk after a chargeback problem (2 of 2). Public tender on blended rate or incumbent rebid (7 of 7 lost or wrong-fit). Merger with a global peer at an in-house orchestrator (2 of 2).

Not a trigger: checkout replatform appears only in average customers (3).

After the deal, an expansion signal: 8 of 17 best customers announced new markets after close. No average customer had any signal after close.

Gaps and checks

What the data doesn't say, and where sources disagree. Read before building campaigns on this.

Blank on purpose

  • Farrow Play (ACC-0018): one contact on file (the champion). No decision maker, no call, interview or review. Intent stage blank.
  • Skerry Interactive (ACC-0055): the one best customer championed by a Head of E-commerce rather than a payments leader. No signal before pipeline, no words on file. Why it worked is unknown.
  • Dalby Invest (ACC-0040): no public signal before pipeline. The only signal on file (fraud analytics hiring) came two years after.
  • Intent stage is blank for 7 of 60 accounts.

Where the sources disagree

  • Belmar Play: Hugo Muller says the brand is live in "about 60 markets". The CRM says 48. It may have grown after a licence round, or one of the two is wrong.
  • Jarrow Holidays: the interview names a PSP renewal as the trigger ("up in six months"). The enrichment file only has the acquisition.
  • Clyde Play: the interview names three new markets in one quarter. The enrichment file has no market launch before pipeline.
  • So the enrichment file misses triggers the customers name themselves. Trigger counts here are a floor.

Limits

  • Synthetic and circular. The data was generated from assumptions, so the ICP confirms them.
  • Small sample. 17 best customers, 6 look-alikes. Every anti-ICP group except tenders has fewer than five accounts: treat them as anecdotes until more deals close.
  • In-sample numbers. The 74% list precision, and the 16 of 16 once a payments leader is required, are fitted and measured on the same 60 accounts. Expect lower on new accounts.
  • Confidence tiers. Acquisitions and market launches are Confirmed. PSP renewals are mostly Inferred, from job postings and analyst notes: verify before acting on them.
  • Buying roles are rep-assigned. Use titles, not roles, when building the list.

Phone calls stay irreplaceable. This tells you who to call, not what they'll say.
